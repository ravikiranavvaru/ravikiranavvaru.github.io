01

The work behind the numbers

The same question has shaped every transformation engagement across a 25‑plus year path that began in financial‑market infrastructure at BSE and moved through Oman LNG, ABB, Toyota Kirloskar Motors, Apollo Tyres, and TVS Sundaram Clayton: how do you make technology create real value on a factory floor — not just on a dashboard?

The answer has stayed consistent across automotive, tyre, industrial, and energy environments. Technology does not create value on its own. Disciplined process thinking, applied before technology is deployed, creates value — technology amplifies it.

A broken process running on new technology is simply a faster, more expensive broken process.

This is a practical position, tested across ERP modernisation, IoT deployments, private 5G rollouts, predictive maintenance programmes, cybersecurity transformations, and data governance work across India, APAC, the Middle East, and Europe — and it led to the D³ Framework™, a methodology for manufacturing digital transformation grounded in Toyota Production System principles.

Genchi genbutsu — go and see — applied to a transformation programme means walking the actual process before selecting technology. Value Stream Mapping before ERP configuration. Standard Work before IoT deployment. Kaizen discipline before AI is introduced. That sequence is what most programmes skip, and what determines whether an investment creates measurable value or simply digitises existing waste at greater speed and cost.

02

The D³ Framework™

A structured approach to manufacturing digital transformation, developed over 25 years of practical work.

D
Data

Data governance and master-data discipline established before any technology deployment. Clean, structured data is the foundation analytics and AI sit on — without it, analytics produces noise and dashboards produce false confidence.

D
Discipline

Process discipline, rooted in Toyota Production System thinking, applied before technology is selected. Value stream mapping, waste elimination, Standard Work. The flow is fixed first; the code comes second.

D
Digitalisation

Technology deployed on a clean process and data foundation — ERP, IoT, industrial AI, private 5G, and cybersecurity controls amplifying real value instead of digitising existing waste.
